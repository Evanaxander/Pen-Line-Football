HOW TO PLAY

  • Flick: press anywhere on the screen, pull back like a catapult and let go. Your pen shoots the other way. The dotted arrow shows roughly how far it will go.
  • Near the edge of the screen? Pull from anywhere — or hold your finger at the edge to charge up to full power.
  • Cancel: slide back to where you started, tap with a second finger, or press Esc.
  • Pens are wild! Lines curve and wobble, just like on paper.
  • Where your line stops is the new ball spot. Then the other side flicks from the same spot.
  • Goal: get your line over the other team's goal line between the posts. Hit a post and the line bounces off!
  • Out: off the side = ball goes back and the other side flicks. Off the end = goal kick or corner.
  • Tackled / Saved: touch an opponent and the ball stops there.
  • Pass: reach a teammate and the ball stops at their feet. Flick again — up to 2 bonus flicks a turn.
  • Move a player: before you flick, drag one of your players (the ones with a dashed ring). A player can stride up to a set distance each turn, so he can work his way anywhere on the pitch. Two limits stop goal-hanging defences: nobody stands in their own six-yard box, and only one outfield player at a time may defend inside his own penalty box. Changed your mind? Tap Undo move.
  • 2 vs 2: teammates take turns flicking, one after the other — on one device, or online with 4 friends sharing one code.
