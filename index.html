HOW TO PLAY

  • Flick: press anywhere, pull back like a catapult and let go. Your pen shoots the other way. Pull further for a harder flick.
  • Pens are wild! Lines curve and wobble, just like on paper.
  • Where your ink line stops is the new ball spot. Then the other player flicks from the same spot.
  • Goal: get your line over the other team's goal line, between the posts.
  • Out: line off the side = ball goes back, other player flicks. Off the end = goal kick or corner.
  • Tackled / Saved: touch an opponent and the ball stops right there.
  • Pass: run your line through a teammate and you get a bonus flick (once per turn).
  • Move a player: before you flick, you can drag one of your players a short way (once per turn).
